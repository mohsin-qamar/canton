Our craft

From loom to lining,
under one roof.

BATIK has spent decades supplying woven, ecru, dyed, printed and embroidered cloth to both the traditional Pakistani market and the western garment industry. Batik Vogue is what happens when we cut that cloth ourselves.

01

Woven & ecru

Greige cotton, cotton-linen and viscose blends woven to our own construction and width.

02

Dyed

Piece-dyeing in a low-water house — indigo, soga brown, olive and clay across the season card.

03

Printed

Hand-drawn batik tulis and cap stamping alongside rotary and screen print for repeat runs.

04

Embroidered & cut

Multi-head embroidery, then graded, cut and finished in our own western-wear line.

For brands & buyers

We also make it for you.

The same line that produces Batik Vogue runs OEM and ODM programmes for western wear labels — your tech pack on our cloth, or ours developed to your brief.

Request the line sheet

OEM production

You bring the tech pack, grading and trims. We source, cut, sew, finish and pack to your spec.

ODM development

Pick from our western-wear blocks and print library; we develop the style and hand over a costed sample.

Private-label prints

Exclusive colourways and repeats drawn for your season, held off the open line for the term agreed.
